For "many companies compared at one point in time" reports (columns = company names, e.g. PNB, Union, BOI... rather than quarters). Paste or import one or more such reports — Ledger finds every period-labeled table inside (e.g. "Cost Ratios Analysis 9MFY26", "Key Fundamentals FY24") and transposes each company into its own proper multi-period bank entry. If a bank with the same name already exists in your list, its data is merged in rather than duplicated.
